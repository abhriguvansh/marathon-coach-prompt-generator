# Coaching Domain Rules

These are durable interpretation rules for MCPG output and review, not a training plan.

- Running mileage is separate from walking mileage. Steps are load context, not walking mileage.
- Climbing is cross-training/strength load. Tennis adds lateral, lower-body, and cardiovascular load.
- Strength/cardio device profiles can be ambiguous; the athlete's direct classification overrides the device classification.
- High-step days increase total load even without recorded walking mileage.
- Heat and humidity change effort interpretation; pace alone is not enough.
- Pain and gait concerns outrank pace or mileage. Mild soreness (0-1) is usually controlled unless paired with pain, gait change, unusual fatigue, or persistence.
- Do not cram missed mileage into later days. Travel interruptions call for conservative rebuilding.
- For beginners, durability and consistency outrank speed work.
- Do not claim marathon-goal readiness from one week. A 4:30 goal remains a stretch goal until sufficient training evidence supports it.
- Before relying on generated check-ins for a new workout structure, confirm that the written structure is parseable.
